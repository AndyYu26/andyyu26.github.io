02 / Practical notesVideo workflows
NOTE 001

Transparency is a format decision.

Removing the background is one step. Keeping an alpha channel through export and into the next editor is another. In BGChanger's Video BG Remover, these export choices behave differently:

  • WebM · VP9Transparent output
  • MOV · ProRes KSTransparent output
  • MKV · VP9Transparent output
  • Standard MP4Solid background

Check alpha support in the receiving editor or player. A black preview alone does not establish whether transparency is present.

NOTE 002

A new scene starts with clear direction.

Background removal separates the subject from its surroundings. Scene editing goes further: a prompt describes the desired setting, and an optional reference image gives the model additional visual direction.

Original clip→Prompt + optional reference→Review

A useful prompt describes the environment, lighting, and mood. Review subject edges, motion, and consistency in the result; generated scenes and processing times vary with the input.

This is a different workflow from exporting a transparent cutout for compositing in another editor.
